Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Prabhaav ("we", "us") is an influencer and creator marketing agency based in Pune, India. This policy explains what personal data we collect through prabhaav.co and our emails, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Brands: your name, brand name, work email, phone or WhatsApp number, website or Instagram, and the campaign details you share with us (such as budget, platform, timeline and target audience).
  • Creators: your name, email, phone or WhatsApp number, social media handle and platform, and details you share with us such as your niche, audience, rates and availability. We may also note publicly available information from your public profile, such as follower count and engagement.
  • Emails: the messages you send us and our replies.

We do not ask for bank, UPI, PAN or Aadhaar details by email or through this website. When payment details are needed for a campaign, we collect them separately and only for that purpose.

Why we use it

  • To contact you about Prabhaav and the campaigns or collaborations you asked about.
  • To match brands with suitable creators. Brands see creator profiles anonymously (niche, platform, city, followers, engagement) until a campaign is confirmed.
  • To run campaigns: briefs, content approvals, invoices and payouts.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting requirements.

We use software, including AI tools, to help us draft emails and suggest creator matches. A person on our team reviews matches and important decisions.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers that help us run Prabhaav (such as email, spreadsheet, database, website hosting and automation services), and, for confirmed campaigns, between the brand and the creators involved, as needed to deliver the work. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as needed to work with you, and afterwards only as long as required for legal, tax or accounting reasons. You can ask us to delete it at any time (see below).

Your choices and rights

  • Stop hearing from us: reply "no thanks" or "unsubscribe" to any email and we will not contact you again.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time.

To make a request or raise a concern, email hello@prabhaav.co. We aim to respond within 7 working days.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the page loads.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
