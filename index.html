About us

An influencer marketing agency built to cost less and fit better

Prabhaav is an influencer and creator marketing agency based in Pune. We work with a network of 100+ creators and connect brands of every size, from local businesses to national labels, with creators whose audiences actually match what the brand sells.

Most agencies charge monthly retainers and carry large teams, and brands pay for that overhead. We built our own matching and outreach system so a small team can do the work of a big one. That keeps our costs low, and we pass the savings on to you.
